Legend: what the columns mean

Every roster and pick, valued by FantasyCalc dynasty trade values. Updated 2026-10-18.

#
Rank by portfolio value, highest first.
Manager
Click a name for that manager's portfolio.
Portfolio value
The combined value of every player and future pick the manager owns. The bar compares managers with each other (shortest to longest), not against zero.
Trend
The portfolio's value at each recent update, as a tiny line (up to the last 14). It fills in as updates accumulate; a dash means fewer than two so far. Hover for the change.
30-day move
How much the market moved the value of the players and picks held right now over the last 30 days. Trades do not change it.
On IR
The share of the portfolio value sitting on players who are on IR or listed Out, PUP, Doubtful or Suspended.
Status
Market crash: the 30-day move is -5% or worse, or 25% or more of the value is on IR or out. Correction: down 2.5% or more. Bull run: up 5% or more. Stable: anything else.
