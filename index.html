How to read the Wealth Index

FantasyCalc dynasty values after Week 4. Movement starts once a second week is on file.

Roster value
Players. The combined FantasyCalc dynasty trade value of every player on the roster (superflex, 12 teams, full PPR). Players outside FantasyCalc's top 1,000 count as zero.
Draft capital
Owned picks. The combined FantasyCalc value of every future rookie pick the team owns after trades.
Total wealth
Roster value plus draft capital. This is what the table is ranked by.
MOV
Movement in rank since last week. ▲ 2 = climbed two spots, ▼ 2 = dropped two, — = no change.
Change
Points of total wealth gained or lost since last week. Trades move it as well as the market.

Legend: what the columns mean

Every roster and pick, valued by FantasyCalc dynasty trade values. Updated 2026-10-10.

#
Rank by portfolio value, highest first.
Manager
Click a name for that manager's portfolio.
Portfolio value
The combined value of every player and future pick the manager owns. The bar compares managers with each other (shortest to longest), not against zero.
30d move
How much the market moved the value of the players and picks held right now over the last 30 days. Trades do not change it.
On IR
The share of the portfolio value sitting on players who are on IR or listed Out, PUP, Doubtful or Suspended.
Status
Market crash: the 30-day move is -5% or worse, or 25% or more of the value is on IR or out. Correction: down 2.5% or more. Bull run: up 5% or more. Stable: anything else.
