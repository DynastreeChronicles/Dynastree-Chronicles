Autopick Bowl: Painty69 and Johnny4Skins, 18 Each

The commissioner dropped the timer to one minute and a lot of the room left. The CPU made 84 of the 276 picks, about 30 percent of the draft. Painty69 and Johnny4Skins tied for the lead with 18, fumbduckdumbfuck made 14, and Sarge71 and CMac91 made 12 each. CMac91 asked in round 5 whether there were 'seriously 23 rounds,' played through round 11, and then let the robot draft Travis Hunter, whom Sleeper lists as a defensive back in a league with no DB slot. YesChef23 'thought about just taking defenses the rest of the way' and settled for two.

'Holy hell' was the right reaction. CMac91 just declined to have it twelve more times.

Win Now, Figure It Out Later

Painty69 took McCaffrey and Barkley back to back and YesChef23 noted that he was 'drafting all the injury prone dudes.' StealingGas read the room: 'all the old heads... win now figure it out later.' YesChef23 later advised against drafting Aaron Rodgers 'or anyone else that probably going to retire until way later,' and Darkspaces replied 'fuck gonna have to revise my strategy.' Rodgers then turned up as an autopick on fumbduckdumbfuck and was free-agent-moved two days later.

The league's draft philosophy is a man in a hat saying 'one more year, king' about a quarterback who is not his.
