The season

1-3, the 1.03, and a pivot on the table

StealingGas is 11th at 1-3 with 446.4 points, 12th in the league in scoring. The high was 152.42 in Week 3 and the low 77.18 in Week 2. 19 transactions so far (1 trade), $402 spent on the wire, $98 left, and the rookie pick sits at 1.03. Lineup efficiency: 83.8%.

StealingGas opened the year with the league's worst score at 83.32, repeated it with 77.18 in Week 2, and announced the tank. Then he spent $99 on a waiver dart, followed it with $298 on Jaxson Dart, and in Week 3 posted a perfect 152.42 lineup to beat Darkspaces by 83. Week 4 brought a 133.5 loss to Johnny4Skins and a 1-3 record.

He is also the league's most active seller of his own roster. The London deal with Hades9mm was the real move: he took Drake London, Taylen Green and a 2027 1st, and sent Quentin Johnston, Cam Ward, TreVeyon Henderson and a 2027 3rd the other way. Week 4 was small change: Sampson and Kendre Miller from free agency, and Eli Raridon for $1 in place of Kyler Murray.

The chat bits stay reliable and the box scores stay confusing. His 446.42 points are the lowest in the league, and his MAXPF is the third-lowest, which keeps the 1.03 in reach. Then he told the chat he would see how the team looks in a few weeks, and that a pivot may end poorly for him. The desk is taking notes.

Desk bio, written after Issue 5, through Week 4. The season line above updates itself.
