The season

Commissioner, landlord, lead negotiator

Darkspaces is 6th at 2-1 with 339.1 points, 11th in the league in scoring. The high was 137.52 in Week 1 and the low 69.16 in Week 3. 14 transactions so far (3 trades), $496 spent on the wire, $104 left, and the rookie pick sits at 1.01. Lineup efficiency: 86.8%.

The draft set the tone. Darkspaces cut the pick timer to a minute, loaded up on receivers, and handed nicknames to twenty-three of his own players, then spent the first waiver run buying a tight end he had not drafted for $101 and Singleton for $301 on top of it. That is $402 gone before the first Monday night, and it has not slowed him down.

The pattern since is consistent: spend big, sell high, talk loudly. He sold Deebo Samuel to Snipe58 for a 2027 2nd, swapped Colston Loveland for Jake Ferguson, then flipped Matthew Stafford to the same buyer for two future 2nds and $100 of FAAB. Along the way he put Maye, Achane and Swift on the block and let the chat watch the price fall from a first to free and back to a first.

The results are mixed. Two wins to start, then a 69.16 against StealingGas's perfect 152.42, the worst score of Week 3. The 2027 pick is the quiet subplot: the new MAXPF rules hand the 1.01 to the team with the weakest best-possible lineup, and Darkspaces currently owns that spot. The desk will note that the commissioner wrote the rule and is leading the race to benefit from it.
