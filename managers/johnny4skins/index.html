The season

Eighteen autopicks, $500 untouched, 3-1

Johnny4Skins is 3rd at 3-1 with 569.0 points, 4th in the league in scoring. The high was 158.46 in Week 3 and the low 125.28 in Week 1. 1 transactions so far (0 trades), $0 spent on the wire, $500 left, and the rookie pick sits at 1.10. Lineup efficiency: 85.6%.

Johnny4Skins took Ja'Marr Chase and Lamar Jackson in the first two rounds, then, apparently, went to dinner. It keeps working. The roster is dual-QB by design or by accident. The honest answer is that it does not matter until an injury forces a decision. He also has a new handle: the account was Cassellrole until October. Next up: Darkspaces.
