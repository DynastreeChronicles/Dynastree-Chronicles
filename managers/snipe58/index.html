The combined FantasyCalc value of every player on the roster plus every future pick owned. Players outside FantasyCalc's top 1,000 count as zero.
League rank

30-day market move
How much the market moved the value of the players and picks held right now over the last 30 days, in points and percent. Trades do not change it.

Value sitting on players who are on IR or listed Out, PUP, Doubtful or Suspended, and the share of the portfolio that is.
Draft capital
The combined value of the future picks this manager owns after trades. Picks use FantasyCalc's own pick values. For the next draft the tier (early, mid or late) comes from the original owner's current draft slot.
League average

Status flag

The season

Three trades, three veterans, and 2-2

Snipe58 is 9th at 2-2 with 451.6 points, 11th in the league in scoring. The high was 121.00 in Week 1 and the low 97.46 in Week 2. 3 transactions so far (3 trades), $0 spent on the wire, $400 left, and the rookie pick sits at 1.02. Lineup efficiency: 85.9%.

Snipe58 does not post much, but the ledger is loud. In four weeks he has bought Deebo Samuel for a 2027 2nd, traded Loveland for Ferguson, and paid a 2028 2nd, a 2029 2nd and $100 of FAAB for Stafford. Each deal was a present-tense purchase funded with future capital.

The roster has the pieces. Justin Jefferson was the Week 1 MVP at 31.2, Brock Purdy was the Week 3 MVP at 31.28 and still lost to Johnny4Skins, and Quinshon Judkins led Week 4 at 21.6. With Stafford on the roster too, he has the dual-QB problem the desk has been writing about.

Week 4 was the win he needed: 112.22 over YesChef23 by 11.76, with 21.28 left on the bench. The record is 2-2, ninth in the standings, and the second-lowest MAXPF gives him the 1.02, which makes every future sell-off look reasonable and every buy look expensive.
