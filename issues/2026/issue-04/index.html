From the desk

Week 3 is in the books and the board is louder than the scoreboard. StealingGas and Hades9mm cleared the London deal: Drake London, Taylen Green, and a 2027 1st to the tank; Quentin Johnston, Cam Ward, TreVeyon Henderson, and a 2027 3rd the other way. Darkspaces flipped Matthew Stafford to Snipe58 for a 2028 2nd, a 2029 2nd, and $100 FAAB, then immediately parked Achane and Swift on the block and negotiated a first in public chat. Zygon listed Emmett Johnson and Blake Corum with the classic "make me regret it when Walker tears his ACL" disclaimer.

Commish (Darkspaces) flipped the 2027 rookie draft to MAXPF linear and turned vetoes on, so tanking just got more expensive. Meanwhile StealingGas posted a perfect lineup for 152.42 and beat Darkspaces by 83 points while still calling it a tank. C33DeezNutzz, Johnny4Skins, and YesChef23 all moved to 2-1. Sarge71 and Painty69 sit 0-3. The desk has receipts.
