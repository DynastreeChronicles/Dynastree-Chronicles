From the desk

Week 6 took the 1.01 away from Darkspaces and handed it to Snipe58, who lost the lowest-scoring game of the week and still came out with the first pick. StealingGas posted a perfect lineup for the second week in a row, 180.64 with nothing left on the bench, and slid from the 1.04 to the 1.05 for it. YesChef23 beat Darkspaces by 0.56 points while Omar Cooper's 22.3 sat on the Darkspaces bench.

At the top, Zygon is 5-1 and Hades9mm holds the league's best MAXPF by 0.02 points, 1032.96 to 1032.94. Johnny4Skins lost to CMac91 and sits at 4-2, and six teams are stacked at 3-3. The chat was nearly silent: StealingGas on Mahomes' leg, a poll that went two votes each to Snipe58 and Darkspaces, and not one word about a trade.

Hero and zero of the week

Hero: StealingGas, perfect lineup

180.64, the best score in the league and a perfect lineup for the second week in a row, with 0.0 left on the bench. Malik Nabers led at 28.5 and Trevor Lawrence went for 28.34 as the QB of the week in a 105.82-point win over Sarge71. Up two spots to 5th.

Perfect lineups keep winning games and losing draft slots. The 1.05 is the invoice.

Zero: fumbduckdumbfuck, 90.72

90.72, 68% of a perfect lineup, with 43.4 left on the bench. Lost to Zygon by 32.08 and fell two spots to 7th.

The bench left behind more than the margin of defeat. The Sleeper report put it politely, and the scoreboard did not.
