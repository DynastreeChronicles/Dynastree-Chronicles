From the desk

Week 5 broke both of last issue's headlines. Zygon, the only unbeaten team, lost the Match of the Week to C33DeezNutzz, and Painty69, the only winless team, beat Hades9mm. Zygon and Johnny4Skins now share the top at 4-1. The rest of the week belonged to StealingGas: a perfect lineup, 202.54 with nothing left on the bench, the best score in the league and a 114.4-point win over YesChef23.

MAXPF, the points each week's best possible lineup would have scored, added up, sets the 2027 rookie draft order, which is why a perfect week is not all good news. StealingGas's best lineup is now worth more, his slot slid from the 1.03 to the 1.04, and YesChef23 holds the 1.03. At the top, Darkspaces set a second straight perfect lineup, 140.36 with nothing left on the bench, and lost again, this time by 23.2 to Johnny4Skins. He holds the 1.01 by 7.06 points of MAXPF over Snipe58. No block player has moved, and the chat spent the week on food poisoning, a gif of a nuclear bomb, and a commissioner who says his IR slot is staying put.

Hero and zero of the week

Hero: StealingGas, perfect lineup

202.54, the best score in the league and a perfect lineup with 0.0 left on the bench. Puka Nacua led at 37.7 in a 114.4-point win over YesChef23. Up four spots to 7th.

The tank announced a pivot and then played it as a perfect lineup. The 1.04 is the receipt.

Zero: CMac91, 74.52

74.52, the lowest score of the week, with 47.98 left on the bench. Lost to fumbduckdumbfuck by 47.46 and fell three spots to 8th.

The bench held more than the margin of defeat. Last week's 183.42 turned out to be a visit, not a move.
