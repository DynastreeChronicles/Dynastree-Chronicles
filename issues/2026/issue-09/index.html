From the desk

Hades9mm did what he told the chat he would do. He beat Darkspaces 124.02 to 67.46 in the Match of the Week, a margin of 56.56, with Mike Gesicki leading at 17.6 and 28.62 left on his bench. Darkspaces, who had asked why his players missed the tanking memo, got the memo: Drake Maye led at 22.46, 15.0 stayed on the bench, and he fell to 3-5 and 9th. His 1056.38 MAXPF (the points a perfect lineup would have scored each week, added up, lowest picks first) still holds the 1.03, 63.26 above Snipe58 at the 1.01. He also sold D'Andre Swift to StealingGas for a 2027 1st, the first block listing to move.

StealingGas posted the top score in the league for the second week running, 173.6 against C33DeezNutzz's 119.94, with Bhayshul Tuten at 29.9 and 18.6 left on the bench. He is 5-3 and 2nd, and the week also had him buy two running backs with picks: Jonah Coleman from Snipe58 for K. Miller and his 2027 2nd, and Swift for his 2027 1st. Zygon is 7-1 with the highest MAXPF, 1417.26, and the 1.12. fumbduckdumbfuck beat Snipe58 164.3 to 62.62 for the biggest margin of the week, and Painty69 left 93.88 on the bench in a 49.42-point loss to Zygon, which put his 1339.8 MAXPF at the 1.11.

164.3 and a 101.68-point win over Snipe58, the biggest margin of the week. Tony Pollard led at 28.7, Brock Bowers was the TE of the week at 28.2, and 18.1 stayed on the bench. Up two spots to 4th at 5-3.

The bench scored 56.3 against the starters' 62.62. He keeps the 1.01 at 993.12 MAXPF, with Sarge71 44.6 above him, and Zygon is next.
