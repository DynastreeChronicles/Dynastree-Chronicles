From the desk

Week 7 ended the perfect-lineup run at two. StealingGas won the Match of the Week over Hades9mm, 157.4 to 145.06, and posted the league's top score, but 18.8 stayed on his bench, so there is no third perfect lineup. He climbed three spots to 2nd at 4-3, and his MAXPF of 1092.08 moved the 1.05 to the 1.08. Zygon beat CMac91 by 33.98 to reach 6-1 and took the highest MAXPF, 1211.42, which makes the 1.12 his and the 1.11 Hades9mm's.

Darkspaces beat Sarge71, 144.34 to 65.88, for the biggest margin of the week at 78.46, and then asked the chat why his players had not gotten the tanking memo. He rose three spots to 8th at 3-4 and still holds the 1.03, 79.02 MAXPF points behind Snipe58 at the 1.01. Hades9mm offered to take the players if he needs to tank, and StealingGas said his own plan is to pop off for a few weeks and go dormant for the playoffs. After a nearly silent Week 6 the chat was back, with trade offers, three quarterbacks out for Johnny4Skins, and a manager addressing the desk as the AI.

Hero and zero of the week

Hero: StealingGas, 157.40

157.4, the best score in the league and a 12.34-point win over Hades9mm in the Match of the Week. Geno Smith led at 24.48, 18.8 stayed on the bench, and the run of perfect lineups stopped at two. Up three spots to 2nd.

The plan was to tank and the result was second place. The draft rule answered by moving him to the 1.08.

Zero: Sarge71, 65.88

65.88, the lowest score in the league, with 14.98 left on the bench. Lost to Darkspaces by 78.46, the biggest margin of the week, and stayed 12th at 1-6.

Bijan Robinson led at 17.2 and nobody else got close. The market feed has him on crash watch and the scoreboard agrees.
