From the desk

Zygon has clinched a spot in the six-team winners bracket at 10-1, and Painty69 (2-9) and Sarge71 (1-10) are out of it. Seeds are still open: Hades9mm is 8-3, StealingGas, fumbduckdumbfuck and CMac91 are 7-4, Johnny4Skins holds the sixth seat at 6-5, and C33DeezNutzz and YesChef23 sit one game back at 5-6. The draft side moved just as fast. MAXPF, the points a best possible lineup would have scored each week, added up (the lowest total picks first in the 2027 rookie draft), puts Sarge71 at 1322.62, Snipe58 at 1385.44 (62.82 behind) and Darkspaces at 1400.94 (78.32 behind). Painty69 is out of the bracket with the second-highest MAXPF in the league, 1833.26.

Hades9mm sent Travis Kelce, DJ Moore and nine picks to Zygon for Tee Higgins and Harold Fannin Jr., and the market now values his picks at 0. The same week Zygon beat Darkspaces 99.12 to 86.18 with 68.62 on his own bench, C33DeezNutzz posted the top score, 155.9, and fumbduckdumbfuck beat Johnny4Skins by 57.02 in the Match of the Week.

Hero and zero of the week

Hero: C33DeezNutzz, perfect lineup

155.9, the top score of the week and a 49.54-point win over YesChef23, one week after a 55.26-point loss. Justin Herbert scored 22.44 and the lineup was the best possible one, 155.9 to 155.9. Sleeper's projection was 138.17 and he beat it by 13%.

He is 5-6, seventh, and one game behind the sixth seat. Sarge71 is next, and the schedule has finally been kind to him.

Zero: Johnny4Skins, 67.98

67.98, the lowest score in the league and a 57.02-point loss to fumbduckdumbfuck in the Match of the Week. Chuba Hubbard scored 26.0 and Sleeper's 123.14 projection was missed by 45%.

Lamar Jackson and Jalen Hurts are on IR, A.J. Brown is Doubtful, and he holds the sixth seat at 6-5 with Zygon next.
