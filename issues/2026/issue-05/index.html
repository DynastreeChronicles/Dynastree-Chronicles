From the desk

The storm passed. Draft hype and early-season noise carried the league through four weeks, and Week 4 arrived with a thin chat log, a scoreboard doing most of the talking, and ten games left before the playoffs. Teams are still searching for an identity, and the block shows it: Darkspaces listed Nico Collins and pitched Swift for a first, and YesChef23 posted George Pickens. No trade cleared this week. The deadline will change that.

On the field, Darkspaces set a perfect lineup, 118.36 with nothing left on the bench, and lost by 65.06 to CMac91's 183.42, the high score of the week. A perfect lineup is only as good as the roster behind it, and MAXPF, the points of each week's best possible lineup added up, now gives Darkspaces the league's lowest total and the 1.01. Zygon beat Hades9mm and is the only unbeaten team. Painty69 lost by 1.34 and is the only winless one.

Hero and zero of the week

Hero: CMac91, 183.42

183.42, the high score of the week, and a 65.06-point win over Darkspaces. CeeDee Lamb went for 41.3 and only 5.52 sat on the bench. Up three spots to 5th.

Zero dollars of FAAB, 183 points of response. The depth collector spent the points instead.

Zero: YesChef23, 100.46

Lowest score of the week at 100.46. Left 38.2 points on the bench, lost to Snipe58 by 11.76, and slid three spots to 8th.

Three claims and $14 of FAAB did not fix a lineup that left points on the pine.
